Licence for every file: **Unsplash License** (https://unsplash.com/license), free for commercial use, no permission or attribution required. Credit is given on `/credits/`.
Originals were fetched at 2400 px wide, then saved as WebP at 1920 px (`name.webp`) and 640 px (`name-sm.webp`) in `public/images/photo/`. No AI-generated images were added.
