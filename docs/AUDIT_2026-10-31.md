# Full audit, 2026-10-31

## Counts (one source of truth)

The earlier report of 69 vs 60 tools, 47 vs 37 verified prices and 15 vs 12 categories no longer reproduces: Home, Directory, Pricing, Methodology
and the sitemap all read `src/data/tools.ts`, which is built from `content/tools/*.json` by `scripts/catalog-lib.mjs`.
Current published records: **69 tools, 15 categories, 47 fully verified prices (22 partial), 30 blog articles (28 guides plus 2 comparison articles that live
under /compare/), 17 news stories.** `tests/consistency.test.ts` now fails the build if any of these drift apart: record count vs files, unique ids and names,
no empty category, verified records carry an https source and a check date, and (when `out/` exists) the numbers printed on the built Home, Directory, Pricing,
Blog and News pages and the sitemap entries match the data.

## Static audit (`node scripts/audit-out.mjs`, after a build)

323 pages and 18,362 internal references: no broken link or asset, one `h1` per page, unique titles and descriptions, canonical on every page, alt text on
every image, valid JSON-LD, no duplicate ids, every `target=_blank` link has `noopener`. The 32 legacy redirect stubs and the 404 page are excluded
(the stubs carry a canonical to the new address).

## Live runtime audit (headless Chrome, 27 routes x desktop and mobile)

All routes returned 200 (the 404 test route returned 404), no console errors or warnings, no hydration errors, no failed requests, no broken images after
scrolling, no horizontal overflow at 375 px.

## Pricing re-check against the official pages (47 verified records)

* 27 records: every monthly and annual price found in the text of the cited page by script.
* 11 records needed a real browser (script could not read them, prices behind a billing toggle, or the page shows fewer plans at narrow widths).
  Checked in the browser: Otter, Synthesia, Zapier (Pay monthly toggle), Consensus (Monthly toggle), Lovable, Gamma, Ideogram, Midjourney, Firefly (all four plans
  appear at desktop width), ElevenLabs and Luma (the one unmatched figure on each is the annual total divided by 12).
* 9 records have no numeric price (free or contact sales).
* Result: no price had to be changed and no record had to be downgraded. Not re-read: free-plan and limit wording, and the 22 partial records.

## Security

`npm audit`: 0 vulnerabilities (production and dev). No secrets in the repository; only `.env.example` is tracked. Dependencies: React 19, Next 16,
three, @react-three/fiber and drei (OrbitControls only). Framer Motion is not a dependency of this project (animations are CSS), so there was nothing to remove.

## Performance experiments this round

* Suspense (selective hydration) boundaries on Home, Blog and the footer: local trace showed the largest hydration task falling from about 500 ms to about 300 ms, but
  live Lighthouse got worse (Home median 68 vs 70, TBT median 1,316 vs 1,269 ms; Blog 64 vs 69, TBT 844 vs 643 ms). Reverted.
