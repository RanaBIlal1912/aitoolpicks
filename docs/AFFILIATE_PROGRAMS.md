# Affiliate programme research

Researched and re-checked on 1 November 2026. **Nothing has been applied for.** Terms change without notice: confirm every figure on the programme's own page or dashboard before you rely on it.

How to read the tables:

- **Official** means the figure was read on the programme's own page or terms during this research.
- **Unverified** means it comes only from third-party directories or search summaries, or the official page could not be read (HTTP 403 or 404).
- **Not stated** means the official page does not say. It does not mean "none" and it does not mean "allowed".

## Pakistan: what could and could not be established

- **No programme below states a country restriction for Pakistan**, and none says Pakistan is accepted. Ask the programme if it matters.
- **Payouts are the open question, and nothing about Pakistan is confirmed from an official page.**
  - **PartnerStack:** a search summary of its support article said Pakistan is among countries with bank direct deposit (not foreign-currency accounts or virtual banks). The article itself could not be opened (HTTP 403), so treat this as **unverified**. PayPal and Stripe availability for Pakistan is also unverified. The payout settings inside the PartnerStack dashboard show what your account is offered.
  - **impact.com:** its help pages describe PayPal and bank payouts depending on supported currency. Nothing found confirms or rules out Pakistan.
  - **Wise** (Make's payout method), **FirstPromoter** (Writesonic) and **PayPal** (HeyGen, Descript's old method): Pakistan support was not checked.
  - **Tax forms:** Writesonic and Namecheap ask for a W-8BEN or similar before paying non-US affiliates.
- Before applying, decide how you will be paid (a local bank account that is not a virtual bank, or a payout service you already use) and check it in the network's payout settings.

## Unconfirmed or unclear

- **Canva** (`canva`): its help pages say the affiliate portal runs on Impact, but the affiliate page returned HTTP 403 and every commission or cookie figure found ("up to $36 per Pro sale", 30 days) is third-party. Unverified.
- **Grammarly** (`grammarly`): directories say Impact, a 90-day cookie and conflicting commissions. No figure came from Grammarly's own pages. Unverified.
- **Zapier** (`zapier`): **no confirmed affiliate programme.** A guide quotes a Zapier staff member saying in January 2025 that it does not have one, and the 25% figure in directories is not on any Zapier page. There is a partner programme for agencies. Do not list it until you find a current official page.

## Programmes that do not fit or are not open

- **HeyGen** (`heygen`): Official: 20% / 25% / 30% recurring for 6 months (tiers by conversions), 30-day tracking, PayPal payouts with a $100 minimum, 60-day verification window. **Needs 5,000+ followers on one platform and original video content; blog or SEO-only promotion is not allowed.** Not a fit for this site today.
- **Notion** (`notion`): Official page: up to $50 per activated sign-up plus 20% of year-one revenue, through PartnerStack, but it says the **programme is not accepting new affiliates**.
- **Jasper** (`jasper`): Official page shows a **Solutions Partner programme for agencies** and says independent affiliates and individual promoters are not eligible.

## Suggested order

1. **Writesonic, Surfer:** the clearest official terms (commission, cookie, how to apply) and a good fit for a text site. Check how you will be paid before applying.
2. **Make, ElevenLabs, Hostinger:** good commissions; confirm cookie length and payout rules on the page or in the dashboard.
3. **Synthesia, Descript, Namecheap, Lovable:** lower or one-off rewards, or networks whose Pakistan payout support is unconfirmed.
4. **Canva, Grammarly:** apply only after you can read their current terms.
5. Apply only with the real site URL (`https://ranabilal1912.github.io/aitoolpicks/`). Do not describe approval anywhere until you have it. When a programme approves you, paste the tracking URL into `src/lib/affiliate.ts` for that tool id and update `/affiliate-disclosure/` to list real programmes only.
