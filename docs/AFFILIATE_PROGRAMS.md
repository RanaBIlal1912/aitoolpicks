# Affiliate programme research

Researched on 1 November 2026, corrected after an independent review, and re-checked on 2 November 2026. **Nothing has been applied for.** Terms change without notice: confirm every figure on the programme's own page or dashboard before you rely on it.

How to read the tables:

- **Official** means the figure was read on the programme's own page or terms during this research.
- **Unverified** means it comes only from third-party directories or search summaries, or the official page could not be read (HTTP 403 or 404).
- **Not stated** means the official page does not say. It does not mean "none" and it does not mean "allowed".

## Pakistan: eligibility and payouts

**Eligibility.** No programme's official page lists the countries it accepts. The only country wording found: Hostinger says an application can be rejected for a location outside its "current focus regions"; Descript excludes OFAC-sanctioned countries; Make says anyone with a Make account can join. **No page says Pakistan is eligible or ineligible, so Pakistan eligibility is not verified for any programme.** Ask each programme before applying where it matters.

**Payouts.** One route is now confirmed by an official source: **PartnerStack direct deposit to a Pakistani bank account** (rupees; see the table). Everything else below is unconfirmed for Pakistan. Being able to receive money does not mean a programme will accept you: each programme decides that.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wise account** | **Make (the only route)** | Wise's Pakistan page: residents of Pakistan cannot directly sign up for Wise accounts; a sender abroad can pay a Pakistani bank account through Wise, in rupees. Make requires your own verified Wise account | Official pages for both (fetched). Together they mean Make is probably unusable from Pakistan |
| **PayPal** | Hostinger (option), HeyGen, FirstPromoter (option), Impact (option), PartnerStack (option) | Third-party guides say residents of Pakistan cannot open or receive into a PayPal account, and a 2024 government announcement had no confirmed working result. | **Bank transfer** | Hostinger (min $500 + 3 conversions), FirstPromoter (manual), Impact (electronic transfer, varies by location) | The routes are described; Pakistan is not named | Not verified |
| **PartnerStack direct deposit** (via Airwallex) | **Surfer, ElevenLabs, Descript** | **Confirmed.** PartnerStack's own help article "Configuring payout providers" lists **Pakistan** among the countries where partners can be paid by direct deposit to a bank account. Conditions on that page: the account must be a local bank account (**foreign-currency accounts and virtual banks are not supported**) and the bank address cannot be a PO box; transfers in the country's own currency use local rails with a fee of **$5.00 + 1.30%** for withdrawals up to $999 ($8.00 + 1.25% up to $4,999); arrival in 2-5 business days; identity checks "at any time". Its "How do I get paid?" article adds: **$5 USD minimum to withdraw**; commissions are normally paid out on the 13th of the month after the company approves them (some companies set their own schedule); all available commissions must be withdrawn in one transaction; **tax information must be completed before every withdrawal**; unclaimed commissions are charged late fees after 2 years | **Official** (opened in a browser on 2 Nov 2026): <https://support.partnerstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009377934-Configuring-payout-providers> and <https://support.partnerstack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009501113-How-do-I-get-paid>. The countries list may change; the withdrawal screen in your PartnerStack account shows what is selectable |
| **Stripe** | PartnerStack (option) | PartnerStack says Stripe is available in 50+ countries and links to Stripe's list; it does not say Pakistan is included | Not verified |

Tax forms: Writesonic and Namecheap ask for a W-8BEN or similar before paying non-US affiliates.

**What to do first:** the confirmed route is PartnerStack. To use it you need a **Pakistani rupee bank account in your own name** (not a foreign-currency or virtual-bank account) with a street address that is not a PO box, and your tax information for the withdrawal form. For every other programme, ask for written confirmation that a Pakistan-based publisher can be accepted and paid, and by which method, before you apply or promote.

## Unconfirmed or unclear

- **Canva** (`canva`): its help pages say the affiliate portal runs on Impact, but the affiliate page returned HTTP 403 and every commission or cookie figure found ("up to $36 per Pro sale", 30 days) is third-party. Unverified.
- **Grammarly** (`grammarly`): directories say Impact, a 90-day cookie and conflicting commissions. No figure came from Grammarly's own pages. Unverified.
- **Zapier** (`zapier`): **no confirmed affiliate programme.** A guide quotes a Zapier staff member saying in January 2025 that it does not have one, and the 25% figure in directories is not on any Zapier page. There is a partner programme for agencies. Do not list it until you find a current official page.

## Programmes that do not fit or are not open

- **HeyGen** (`heygen`): Official: 20% / 25% / 30% recurring for 6 months (tiers by conversions), 30-day tracking, PayPal payouts with a $100 minimum, 60-day verification window. **Needs 5,000+ followers on one platform and original video content; blog or SEO-only promotion is not allowed.** Not a fit for this site today.
- **Notion** (`notion`): Official page: up to $50 per activated sign-up plus 20% of year-one revenue, through PartnerStack, but it says the **programme is not accepting new affiliates**.
- **Jasper** (`jasper`): Official page shows a **Solutions Partner programme for agencies** and says independent affiliates and individual promoters are not eligible.

## Suggested order

This ranks fit and the clarity of published terms. **Only the PartnerStack programmes have a confirmed payout route to a Pakistan-based publisher**; being paid is not the same as being accepted, so each still decides on your application.

1. **Surfer and ElevenLabs** (one PartnerStack account covers both): clear terms, the strongest commissions here, and a confirmed bank-payout route to Pakistan. Surfer wants AI, SEO or content sites with a public presence and answers within about 24 hours.
2. **Writesonic:** clear terms, no traffic minimum, approval usually within 24 hours, but it pays through FirstPromoter and its page names no payout method, so ask which methods work for Pakistan first.
3. **Hostinger:** good commission, but its PayPal route is likely unavailable from Pakistan and the bank route needs $500 and 3 sales; ask whether Pakistan is inside its "focus regions".
4. **Descript, Synthesia, Namecheap, Lovable:** lower or one-off rewards, or payout support for Pakistan unconfirmed (Descript is payable via PartnerStack but pays a one-time $25).
5. **Make:** best commission among these, but its only payout route is Wise, which is closed to Pakistan residents. Treat as unavailable unless you can confirm otherwise.
6. **Canva, Grammarly:** apply only after you can read their current terms.
7. Apply only with the real site URL (`https://ranabilal1912.github.io/aitoolpicks/`). Do not describe approval anywhere until you have it. When a programme approves you, paste the tracking URL into `src/lib/affiliate.ts` for that tool id and update `/affiliate-disclosure/` to list real programmes only.
