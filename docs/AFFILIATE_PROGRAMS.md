# Affiliate programme research

Researched, re-checked and corrected after an independent review on 1 November 2026. **Nothing has been applied for.** Terms change without notice: confirm every figure on the programme's own page or dashboard before you rely on it.

How to read the tables:

- **Official** means the figure was read on the programme's own page or terms during this research.
- **Unverified** means it comes only from third-party directories or search summaries, or the official page could not be read (HTTP 403 or 404).
- **Not stated** means the official page does not say. It does not mean "none" and it does not mean "allowed".

## Pakistan: eligibility and payouts

**Eligibility.** No programme's official page lists the countries it accepts. The only country wording found: Hostinger says an application can be rejected for a location outside its "current focus regions"; Descript excludes OFAC-sanctioned countries; Make says anyone with a Make account can join. **No page says Pakistan is eligible or ineligible, so Pakistan eligibility is not verified for any programme.** Ask each programme before applying where it matters.

**Payouts.** Nothing below is confirmed for Pakistan by an official source of the payer.

| Payout route | Used by | What was found | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Wise account** | **Make (the only route)** | Wise's Pakistan page: residents of Pakistan cannot directly sign up for Wise accounts; a sender abroad can pay a Pakistani bank account through Wise, in rupees. Make requires your own verified Wise account | Official pages for both (fetched). Together they mean Make is probably unusable from Pakistan |
| **PayPal** | Hostinger (option), HeyGen, FirstPromoter (option), Impact (option), PartnerStack (option) | Third-party guides say residents of Pakistan cannot open or receive into a PayPal account, and a 2024 government announcement had no confirmed working result. PayPal's own country page could not be read | **Unverified**: third-party sources only |
| **Bank transfer** | Hostinger (min $500 + 3 conversions), FirstPromoter (manual), Impact (electronic transfer, varies by location) | The routes are described; Pakistan is not named | Not verified |
| **PartnerStack direct deposit** | Surfer, ElevenLabs, Descript | PartnerStack's payouts page lists direct deposit, Stripe and PayPal and gives no country list. A search summary of its support article said Pakistan is among supported countries (not foreign-currency accounts or virtual banks); the article returned HTTP 403 and could not be read | **Unverified** |
| **Stripe** | PartnerStack (option) | No country list on PartnerStack's page | Not verified |

Tax forms: Writesonic and Namecheap ask for a W-8BEN or similar before paying non-US affiliates.

**What to do first:** email or ask support at the programme or network for written confirmation that a Pakistan-based publisher can be accepted and paid, and by which method, before you apply or promote.

## Unconfirmed or unclear

- **Canva** (`canva`): its help pages say the affiliate portal runs on Impact, but the affiliate page returned HTTP 403 and every commission or cookie figure found ("up to $36 per Pro sale", 30 days) is third-party. Unverified.
- **Grammarly** (`grammarly`): directories say Impact, a 90-day cookie and conflicting commissions. No figure came from Grammarly's own pages. Unverified.
- **Zapier** (`zapier`): **no confirmed affiliate programme.** A guide quotes a Zapier staff member saying in January 2025 that it does not have one, and the 25% figure in directories is not on any Zapier page. There is a partner programme for agencies. Do not list it until you find a current official page.

## Programmes that do not fit or are not open

- **HeyGen** (`heygen`): Official: 20% / 25% / 30% recurring for 6 months (tiers by conversions), 30-day tracking, PayPal payouts with a $100 minimum, 60-day verification window. **Needs 5,000+ followers on one platform and original video content; blog or SEO-only promotion is not allowed.** Not a fit for this site today.
- **Notion** (`notion`): Official page: up to $50 per activated sign-up plus 20% of year-one revenue, through PartnerStack, but it says the **programme is not accepting new affiliates**.
- **Jasper** (`jasper`): Official page shows a **Solutions Partner programme for agencies** and says independent affiliates and individual promoters are not eligible.

## Suggested order

This ranks fit and the clarity of published terms only. **No programme has a confirmed Pakistan payout route**, so ask first (see the Pakistan section).

1. **Writesonic, Surfer:** clear official commission, cookie and application steps. Surfer pays through PartnerStack; Writesonic through FirstPromoter (PayPal or Wise, with manual bank payouts), so the payout question applies to both.
2. **ElevenLabs, Hostinger:** good commissions. Hostinger's PayPal route is likely unavailable from Pakistan, so only its bank route ($500 and 3 conversions) could apply.
3. **Synthesia, Descript, Namecheap, Lovable:** lower or one-off rewards, or networks whose Pakistan payout support is unconfirmed.
4. **Make:** best commission among these, but its only payout route is Wise, which is closed to Pakistan residents. Treat as unavailable unless you can confirm otherwise.
5. **Canva, Grammarly:** apply only after you can read their current terms.
6. Apply only with the real site URL (`https://ranabilal1912.github.io/aitoolpicks/`). Do not describe approval anywhere until you have it. When a programme approves you, paste the tracking URL into `src/lib/affiliate.ts` for that tool id and update `/affiliate-disclosure/` to list real programmes only.
