# Affiliate programme research

Researched on 1 November 2026. **Nothing has been applied for.** Terms change without notice: confirm every figure on the programme's own page or dashboard before you rely on it.

Each figure is marked **Official** (read on the programme's own page or terms during this research) or **Unverified** (third-party directories, or an official page that could not be read). Where a page said nothing, the cell says "Not stated"; it does not mean "none".

## Pakistan: what could and could not be established

- **No programme below states a country restriction for Pakistan.** Not stated is not the same as accepted, so tick "ask support" on the application where it matters.
- **Payouts are the real question.**
  - **PartnerStack:** its support article lists Pakistan among countries where **direct deposit to a bank account** is available (foreign-currency accounts and virtual banks are not supported). PayPal and Stripe availability for Pakistan could not be confirmed; check the payout settings inside the dashboard.
  - **impact.com:** payout methods are PayPal or bank transfer, depending on supported currency. Nothing found confirms or rules out Pakistan.
  - **Direct programmes (for example HeyGen):** pay by PayPal. PayPal's coverage of Pakistan could not be confirmed.
- Before applying, decide how you will receive money (a local bank account in PKR or USD that is not a virtual bank, or a payout service you already use), and confirm it in the network's payout settings.

## Programmes that do not fit or are not open

- **HeyGen** (`heygen`): Official: 20% / 25% / 30% recurring for 6 months (tiers by conversions), 30-day tracking, PayPal payouts with a $100 minimum, 60-day verification window. **Needs 5,000+ followers on one platform and original video content; blog or SEO-only promotion is not allowed.** Not a fit for this site today.
- **Notion** (`notion`): Official page: up to $50 per activated sign-up plus 20% of year-one revenue, via PartnerStack, but it says the **programme is not accepting new affiliates**.
- **Jasper** (`jasper`): Official page shows a **Solutions Partner programme for agencies**; it says independent affiliates and individual promoters are not eligible.
- **Canva** (`canva`): The official affiliate page returned HTTP 403, so no figures could be verified. Check <https://www.canva.com/affiliates/> yourself.
- **Zapier** (`zapier`), **Make** (`make`): the affiliate pages I tried returned 404; terms were not verified. Search for the current page before applying.

## Suggested order

1. **Surfer, ElevenLabs, Hostinger, Descript:** clear published terms, no stated country restriction, and all on networks (PartnerStack or direct) that list Pakistan bank payouts or a free sign-up.
2. **Synthesia, Writesonic, Lovable, Grammarly, Namecheap:** apply once their current terms are read on the page.
3. Apply only with the real site URL (`https://ranabilal1912.github.io/aitoolpicks/`). Do not describe approval anywhere until you have it. When a programme approves you, paste the tracking URL into `src/lib/affiliate.ts` for that tool id and update `/affiliate-disclosure/` to list real programmes only.
