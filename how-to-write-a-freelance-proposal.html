How to Write a Freelance Proposal That Gets a Reply

Most clients receive many proposals and read only the first few lines. A good proposal is short, specific and about the client's problem, not about you.

The structure that works

  1. Start with their problem. Show you read the job post in one sentence.
  2. Say how you will solve it. Two or three clear steps.
  3. Show one relevant sample. Link to one piece of work that matches the job.
  4. Be clear about price and time. Give a number and a delivery date, or say what you need to know to give one.
  5. End with one easy question. It makes replying simple.

A template to edit

Hi [Name],

I read your post about [their problem]. I can help by [step one], [step two] and [step three].

Here is a similar piece of work: [link].

I can deliver by [date] for [price]. Do you want the final files as [format A] or [format B]?

Thanks,
[Your name]

Replace every bracket with real details. A template is a starting point, not something to send unchanged. Clients notice copy-pasted messages.

Common mistakes

  • Long paragraphs about your life story.
  • Promising results you cannot control.
  • Sending the same message to every job.
  • Spelling mistakes in the first line.
