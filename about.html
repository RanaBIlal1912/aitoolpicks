About AI Tool Picks

AI Tool Picks is a small independent site that explains AI and online tools in plain English for freelancers and people starting a small online business.

How we choose tools

We prefer tools with a free plan, so you can try before you pay. For each tool we say who it suits and who should skip it. We don't publish prices or feature lists we haven't confirmed, and we ask you to check the official site before you buy.

How the site makes money

Some links are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See the affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

Tools change quickly. If something on a page is out of date, tell us and we will fix it.
