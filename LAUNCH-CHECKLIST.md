# Launch checklist (do in this order)

1. **Put the site online (free):** upload the `aitoolpicks` folder to Cloudflare Pages or Netlify (drag and drop). Later buy a domain.
2. **Apply to affiliate programs** (you must do this yourself, with your real details). Check each program's own page for Pakistan eligibility and payout method:
   - Canva, Grammarly, Notion, Jasper, Hostinger, Namecheap: search "<name> affiliate program".
   - Networks that list many programs: Digistore24, PartnerStack, Impact.
   - Most programs want a live site first, so do step 1 before this.
3. **Set up Payoneer** (or Wise) to receive payouts.
4. **Paste approved links** into `site.js` (one place, all pages update).
5. **Keep the disclosure** on every page. It is required by most programs.
6. **Publish 2 new articles per week** for the first 3 months (comparisons, "X vs Y", "best X for Y"). Affiliate sites usually take months to get search traffic.
7. **Share** each article on Pinterest, Reddit communities that allow it, and LinkedIn. Don't spam.
8. **Track:** add Google Search Console (free) after launch.

Income note: affiliate income is slow at first and not guaranteed. Pay tax (FBR) on what you earn.
