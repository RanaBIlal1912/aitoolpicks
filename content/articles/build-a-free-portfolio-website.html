A portfolio site shows clients your best work in one link. You can start for free and add a custom domain later.

Option 1: A free static site

If you can edit a simple HTML page (or ask an AI assistant to help), free static hosts such as GitHub Pages, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages can publish it at no cost. This site is also published as static files on GitHub Pages. It is fast and cheap, but you edit files rather than click in a visual editor.

Option 2: A site builder

Visual builders let you drag and drop. Many have free plans with limits and the builder's name in the address. Check the limits before you commit.

Option 3: Your own domain and hosting

A custom domain such as yourname.com looks more professional and is worth it once you are getting client enquiries.

What to put on your portfolio

  • 3 to 5 of your best samples, with a sentence about each.
  • A short bio and the kind of clients you want.
  • One clear way to contact you.

Make each sample tell a story

For every sample, write three short lines: the problem the client had, what you did, and the result or what you learned. Clients scan a portfolio quickly, and a short story is easier to trust than a bare image. If a sample was a practice project, say so. Never present made-up work as client work.

Step by step

  1. Write your content in a document first so you are not designing around empty boxes.
  2. Choose one of the free options above and publish a single simple page.
  3. Use one readable font, two colours and plenty of space.
  4. Add descriptive text to every image so the page works for screen readers and search.
  5. Open the page on your phone before you share it.

Checklist before you share the link

  • Every link works.
  • Your contact method works and you tested it.
  • The page title says what you do, not just your name.
  • You have permission to show each sample.

When to move to your own domain

A free address is fine to start. Move to your own domain when you regularly send clients to the page and want a more professional address. Check the renewal price before you buy, and read our hosting guide.
