Workflow automation tools connect apps so a trigger in one starts an action in another, and now often add AI steps. They differ most in how they charge (tasks, credits or workflow executions), whether you can host them yourself and how technical you need to be.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

What to compare

  • What counts as one unit of use: a task, a credit and a workflow execution are not the same thing, so equal-looking plans can cost very different amounts.
  • Hosting: n8n's licence is source-available (not open source) and it can be self-hosted; the others are hosted services.
  • Who builds the workflows: no-code builders suit non-developers; developers may prefer code-friendly options.

The tools, side by side

Task-based

Zapier

Zapier connects apps into automated workflows and offers AI agents and chatbots as add-ons, with plans measured in monthly tasks.

Good for: individuals, teams, businesses. Watch out for: Prices rise with the task tier.

Plans (pricing verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Free: free; Professional: $29.99/month monthly, $19.99/month billed yearly; Team: $103.50/month monthly, $69/month billed yearly; Enterprise: custom pricing.

Self-hostable

n8n

n8n is a workflow automation tool with hosted cloud plans and a self-hosted Community Edition on GitHub.

Good for: developers, technical teams. Watch out for: The Business plan is self-hosted only (hosted availability is waitlisted).

Plans (pricing verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Starter: €20/month billed yearly; Pro: €50/month billed yearly; Business: €667/month billed yearly; Enterprise: custom pricing.

How to choose

  • Write down one real workflow and count how many steps and runs it needs per month, then price it on each tool.
  • Check what happens on the free plan when you hit its limit.
  • If you need to keep data on your own servers, check the self-hosting licence terms first.
