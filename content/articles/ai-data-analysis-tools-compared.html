AI analytics tools let you ask questions of data in plain language and get charts and reports back. Hex centres on notebooks and data apps for data teams; Julius is a wider workspace that also builds presentations and websites. Both meter usage, so credits and seats matter.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

Check before you upload data

  • Where your files are stored and for how long (Julius's lower plans list 7-day file storage).
  • Whether connectors reach the systems you actually use.
  • Who in your organisation is allowed to send business data to a third party.

How to choose

  • Try both free plans on one real, non-sensitive dataset.
  • Confirm whether the price shown is monthly or annual before you compare.
  • Verify answers against the raw data: AI analysis can be wrong.
