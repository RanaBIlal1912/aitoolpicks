AI video splits into two kinds. Generators such as Runway, Luma and Pika create new footage from a prompt or an image. Avatar tools such as HeyGen and Synthesia turn a script into a presenter-style video. They solve different problems and are priced differently.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

Which kind do you need?

  • Short creative clips or visual effects: a generator.
  • Training, explainers or product walkthroughs with a presenter: an avatar tool.
  • Editing existing recordings: a video editor, covered separately in the directory.

How to choose

  • Video tools usually meter by credits or seconds, so estimate how many clips you really need.
  • Check the resolution, watermark and commercial-use terms on the plan you would actually buy.
  • For avatars, read the rules on using a real person's likeness or voice.
