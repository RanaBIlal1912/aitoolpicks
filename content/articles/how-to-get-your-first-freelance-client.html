The first client is the hardest because you have no reviews. The plan below keeps it simple: pick one service, make a few samples, then contact people directly.

Step 1: Pick one clear service

"I do everything" is hard to trust. Choose one service for one type of client, for example "product descriptions for small online shops" or "simple logos for local businesses".

Step 2: Make three samples

You do not need clients to make samples. Create three pieces of work for imaginary or real small businesses and put them in a simple portfolio. See how to build a free portfolio website.

Step 3: Set up one profile

Create a profile on one marketplace and write it around the client's benefit. A clear photo, a short description and your three samples are enough to begin.

Step 4: Contact people directly

Make a list of ten small businesses you could help. Send each a short, personal message that mentions one thing you noticed about their business and one idea. Do not send the same message to everyone. Use the structure in our proposal guide.

Step 5: Deliver, then ask for a review

Do the job well, meet the deadline and ask the client for a short testimonial. Reviews and real examples make the second client much easier.

Stay safe

  • Do not pay money to "get" a job. Real clients pay you, not the other way round.
  • Keep communication and payment on the platform you use, when it has protection for both sides.
  • Be careful with offers that sound too good, ask for personal or bank details early, or push you to leave the platform.
