Marketing teams use AI for two separate things: producing copy and optimising it for search. Jasper centres on content for marketing teams; Writesonic and Surfer centre on content and AI search visibility. Treat generated copy as a draft, and check claims before you publish.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

Two jobs

  • Writing: drafts, rewrites and variations for ads, pages and email.
  • SEO: guidance on what to cover and how to structure a page for search.
  • Both need a person: check facts, tone and any claim you would be responsible for.

How to choose

  • Test each on one real brief and compare how much editing the output needs.
  • Check word or credit limits and what happens when you exceed them.
  • Be wary of any tool that promises rankings; no vendor can guarantee them.
