Most AI tools are web services, so what you type leaves your device. Before you use one for anything private, answer a few questions. Vendors differ and change their terms, so this is a checklist to apply to the tool's own documentation, not a verdict on any product.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

The checklist

  • Is my content used to train models? Look for an opt-out. The ChatGPT pricing page, for example, lists an opt-out for each plan; other vendors vary.
  • How long is it kept, and can I delete it?
  • Who at the vendor can read it, and under what conditions?
  • Where is it processed? Region matters for some legal rules.
  • What do team and enterprise plans change? Admin controls and data terms often differ from personal plans.
  • Does my employer or client allow this tool? Check before you paste anything confidential.
  • What is shared by default? Public links, shared workspaces and connected apps widen who can see content.
  • Could I run it locally instead? Open-source models can run on your own hardware, with the costs and upkeep that brings.

Practical habits

  • Remove names, numbers and identifiers you do not need to share.
  • Use the vendor's business plan, not a personal account, for work data where one exists.
  • Keep a list of the tools your team uses and review their terms when they change.

Ollama

Ollama is a tool for running large language models on your own hardware, with optional paid plans for cloud-hosted models. Its source code is published on GitHub under the MIT licence.

Good for: developers, teams. Watch out for: Cloud models are hosted primarily in the United States, with possible routing to Europe.

Plans (pricing verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Pay as you go: free; Pro: $20/month monthly, $16.67/month billed yearly; Max: $100/month; Team (early access): $500/month; Enterprise: custom pricing.

Claude

Claude is Anthropic's AI assistant, used to write, analyse, work through problems and handle longer documents, with separate plans for individuals, teams and enterprises.

Good for: individuals, teams of 2 to 150 users (team plan), large organisations (enterprise). Watch out for: Usage limits apply on every plan and reset on a rolling five-hour window; paid plans add weekly limits.

Plans (pricing verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Free: free; Pro: $20/month monthly, $17/month billed yearly; Max: $100/month; Team (Standard seat): $25 per seat/month monthly, $20 per seat/month billed yearly; Team (Premium seat): $125 per seat/month monthly, $100 per seat/month billed yearly.

ChatGPT

ChatGPT is OpenAI's chat assistant. You describe what you need in plain language and it replies in text, which people use for drafts, outlines, explanations and ideas.

Good for: freelancers, students, small business owners. Watch out for: Can state wrong facts confidently, so check anything important.
