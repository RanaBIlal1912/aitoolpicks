Customer support tools now include AI agents that answer questions before a person does. The key question is what you pay for the AI: a bundled allowance, a charge per conversation or a charge per resolved issue. The three tools below use different models.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

Three ways to be charged

  • Per agent (seat): a price for each human agent, as in Zendesk's plans.
  • Per conversation: plan limits stated as a number of billable conversations, as in Tidio.
  • Per resolution: an amount for each issue the AI resolves, which Zendesk lists beyond an included amount.

Customer platform

Intercom

Intercom is a customer service platform with seat-based plans and an AI agent, Fin, billed per outcome.

Good for: support teams, businesses. Watch out for: Fin has a minimum monthly commitment.

Plans (pricing partly verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Essential: $19 per seat/month; Advanced: $85 per seat/month; Expert: $132 per seat/month; Fin AI Agent: $0.99/month.

Zendesk

Zendesk brings email, messaging, phone and social conversations into one place, with AI agents for resolving service issues across channels.

Good for: support teams, businesses. Watch out for: Priced per agent, and AI resolutions are billed on top of the included amount.

Plans (pricing verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Support Team: $25/month monthly, $19/month billed yearly; Suite Team: $69/month monthly, $55/month billed yearly; Suite Professional: $149/month monthly, $115/month billed yearly; Suite Enterprise + Copilot: custom pricing.

Visit Zendesk

Tidio

Tidio combines AI and human conversations on one platform, with the Lyro AI Agent answering common questions and no-code Flows for conversion paths.

Good for: small businesses, online stores, support teams. Watch out for: Annual prices were not stated on the page we read.

Plans (pricing partly verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Free: free; Starter: $24.17/month; Growth: $49.17/month; Plus: price not captured (see the tool page); Premium: custom pricing.

How to choose

  • Estimate your monthly conversations and how many an AI could realistically resolve, then price each model.
  • Read how each vendor defines a conversation or a resolution before comparing numbers.
  • Check data handling: customer messages are personal data in many places.
