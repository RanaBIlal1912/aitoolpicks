The quality of an AI answer depends on your question. These prompts are starting points for common freelance tasks. Replace the words in brackets, then check and edit every answer yourself.

Tips for better answers

  • Say who the text is for and what you want from them.
  • Give the tone you want (friendly, formal, short).
  • Paste in your real details, not just general instructions.
  • Ask for two or three options, then pick the best.

Prompts you can use

1. Understand a job post

Read this job post and list the client's main problem, what they want delivered, and three questions I should ask before quoting: [paste job post]

2. Draft a proposal

Write a proposal under 150 words for this job. I am a [your skill] with experience in [your experience]. Keep a friendly, professional tone and end with one question: [paste job post]

3. Polite follow-up

Write a short, polite follow-up message to a client who has not replied in a week about [project]. Do not sound pushy.

4. Explain your price

Help me explain to a client why my price for [service] is [price]. Use simple words and avoid sounding defensive.

5. Project outline

Create a simple step-by-step plan for [project], with a rough order of tasks and what I need from the client at each step.

6. Improve your profile

Rewrite this freelance profile so it is clearer and focused on the client's benefit. Keep it under 120 words and do not add claims I did not write: [paste profile]

Rules to protect your reputation

  • Do not paste a client's private information or confidential files into any tool unless you are allowed to.
  • Check facts, names, dates and numbers before sending anything.
  • Check whether your client or platform has rules about using AI in the work you deliver.

A simple prompt recipe

Most good prompts have the same five parts. Use them as a checklist and fill in what you know.

  1. Role: "You are an experienced [type of professional]."
  2. Task: what you want, in one clear sentence.
  3. Context: who it is for, what has already happened, any facts the assistant must use.
  4. Format: length, structure, tone and what to leave out.
  5. Check: ask it to list any assumptions or things it is unsure about.
You are an experienced copywriter. Write a 120-word product description for a handmade leather wallet. It is for gift buyers aged 25 to 40. Use a warm, simple tone, mention it is handmade, and do not invent materials or measurements. Then list any details I should confirm.

Checking what you get back

  • Highlight every name, number, date and claim. Verify each against a real source.
  • Delete filler such as "in today's fast-paced world".
  • Read it aloud. If it does not sound like you, rewrite those lines.
  • Run a final spelling and tone check before sending.

When a prompt does not work

Add the missing context, give a short example of what good looks like, or break the task into two smaller steps. Ask the assistant to ask you questions first if you are not sure what it needs.
