You can start freelancing with almost no budget. This is a small toolkit that covers design, organisation and writing, with free options first.

Design

Canva

Make logos, social posts, proposals and simple presentations from templates. Has a free plan; paid plans add more templates and features.

Good for: non-designers who need clean visuals fast.

Try Canva
Organisation

Notion

Notes, task lists and simple client trackers in one place. A free plan is available for personal use.

Good for: keeping client work, deadlines and proposal templates organised.

Try Notion
Writing

Grammarly

Catches errors in proposals and messages so you look professional from the first message.

Try Grammarly
AI assistant

ChatGPT

Helps draft proposals, outline projects and brainstorm ideas. Always review the result before sending it to a client.

Try ChatGPT

A simple workflow

  1. Create a profile on a freelance marketplace and write a clear service description.
  2. Use an AI assistant to draft proposals, then personalise each one.
  3. Use Canva for a portfolio and Notion to track clients and deadlines.
  4. Run every message through a grammar checker before sending.
