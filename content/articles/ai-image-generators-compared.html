Image generators charge in different ways: monthly GPU time, credits, tokens or a plain subscription. That makes two plans with the same headline price hard to compare, so start with how each one meters usage.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

How they charge

  • GPU time: you buy hours of fast generation per month, with slower unlimited modes on higher plans.
  • Credits or tokens: each image costs a number of credits that depends on the model and settings.
  • Subscription with a generation allowance: a monthly allowance that resets.

Commercial use

Whether you may use generated images in client work depends on the vendor's terms and your plan. Midjourney, for example, publishes a revenue threshold above which a company must use its Pro or Mega plan. Read the terms of any tool before you use its output commercially.

How to choose

  • Generate the same three prompts in each free or cheapest plan and compare results for your style.
  • Work out what a typical month of images costs in credits or GPU time before choosing a tier.
  • Where a record says the billing interval was unclear, confirm it on the official page.
