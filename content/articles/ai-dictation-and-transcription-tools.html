"Speech to text" is three different jobs. Dictation tools type what you say into any app. Meeting tools turn a recorded conversation into notes. Speech APIs let developers add transcription to their own products. The tools below fall into those groups.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

Which job do you have?

  • You want to talk instead of type: a dictation app such as Wispr Flow.
  • You want searchable meeting notes: a notetaker such as Otter.
  • You are building a product: a speech API such as Deepgram or AssemblyAI, priced by usage.

The tools, side by side

Dictation

Wispr Flow

Wispr Flow is a dictation app that turns speech into written text in any app, and it also offers a Notetaker for meetings.

Good for: professionals, students (50% off pro with an education email). Watch out for: Free plan is limited to 2,000 words per week on desktop.

Plans (pricing partly verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Free: free; Pro: $15 per user/month monthly, $12 per user/month billed yearly; Growth: price not captured (see the tool page); Enterprise: custom pricing.

Meeting transcripts

Otter.ai

Otter.ai records and transcribes meetings and conversations, with a free Basic plan and Pro, Business and Enterprise plans priced per user.

Good for: professionals, teams, students (20% off pro with .edu email). Watch out for: Basic has 300 minutes per month, 30 minutes per conversation and 3 lifetime file imports.

Plans (pricing verified, checked 30 Oct 2026): Basic: free; Pro: $16.99 per user/month monthly, $8.33 per user/month billed yearly; Business: $30 per user/month monthly, $19.99 per user/month billed yearly; Enterprise: custom pricing.

Speech API

Deepgram

Deepgram provides speech-to-text, text-to-speech, voice agent and audio intelligence APIs, billed per minute of audio.

Good for: developers, enterprises. Watch out for: Enterprise speech-to-text rates are not stated.

How to choose

  • Test accuracy on your own voice, accent and vocabulary; vendors cannot promise how it will do on yours.
  • For APIs, estimate hours of audio per month before comparing per-hour prices.
  • Check how long audio and transcripts are stored and who can read them.
