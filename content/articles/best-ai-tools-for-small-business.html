How to build your stack

  1. List the three tasks that take you the most time.
  2. Try a free tool for each one.
  3. Keep only what you actually use after a month.

A starter stack, by job

  • Writing: one AI assistant for drafts and ideas, plus a proofreader for customer-facing messages.
  • Design: a template design tool for social posts, flyers and menus.
  • Organisation: one workspace for tasks, customer notes and templates.
  • Website: start with the simplest option that gets you online, then add your own domain.

Rules for using AI in a business

  • Protect customer data. Do not paste customer names, contact details or payment information into a tool unless its terms allow it and you have the right to share it.
  • Check before you publish. Prices, opening hours, claims and legal wording must be correct. An assistant does not know your business.
  • Be honest with customers. If AI helped write something, do not claim a person who does not exist wrote it, and follow any rules in your industry.
  • Keep a human in the loop. Use AI for first drafts and ideas, not for final decisions about people or money.

Budgeting sensibly

  1. Start with free plans for a month.
  2. Write down which task each tool does and how often you use it.
  3. Cancel anything you did not open in the last month.
  4. Upgrade one tool at a time, and only for a feature you can name.
