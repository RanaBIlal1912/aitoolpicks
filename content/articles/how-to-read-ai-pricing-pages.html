AI pricing pages look similar but hide very different rules. A price per month can mean one person, one seat in a team, a number of credits or a slice of GPU time. These examples come from the pricing pages we checked on 30 October 2026.

How this guide was made: it is a documentation-based overview. We read each vendor's official pages and recorded plans, prices and limits on the date shown; we have not run hands-on tests or benchmarks, so nothing here is a performance claim. Prices change, so confirm on the official page before you pay.

Monthly or yearly

Many vendors show a lower price if you pay yearly. Gamma lists its Plus plan at $12 per seat per month billed monthly and $9 per seat per month billed annually ($108). Midjourney says annual billing is a 20% discount paid upfront. Always check which of the two a headline price refers to.

Seats, credits and usage

  • Per seat: Gamma and DeepL's team plans are priced per person.
  • Credits: Ideogram's plans include a number of priority credits per month, and each image costs credits that depend on the model.
  • GPU time: Midjourney's plans include hours of fast GPU time, with extra hours sold at $4 per hour.
  • Per use: Replicate bills by run time or output, for example per output image or per second of hardware, so there is no fixed monthly price.

Free plans and free trials are not the same

A free plan lets you keep using a limited version. A free trial gives full access for a short time and then asks you to pay. DeepL, for example, lists both: a capped free version, plus a 30-day trial on its Individual and Team plans. Our directory filters them separately.

Regional prices

Some vendors show prices in the visitor's local currency. Canva showed its prices in rupees, per year, when we opened the page from Pakistan, and other countries see different amounts. That is why our records keep the vendor's own currency and label the region, instead of converting everything to one number.

A short checklist

  • Is the price per person, per team or per use?
  • Is it billed monthly or yearly, and is tax included?
  • What happens when you hit the limit: slower, blocked or extra charges?
  • Is there a free plan, a trial or neither?
  • Does the page show a date or region? If not, confirm before paying.
