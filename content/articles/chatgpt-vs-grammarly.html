People often compare these two, but they solve different problems. One helps you create text; the other helps you fix it.

The short answer

  • Need ideas, outlines or a first draft? Use an AI assistant like ChatGPT.
  • Already have text and want it clean? Use Grammarly for spelling, grammar and tone.
  • Doing client work? Use both: draft, edit it yourself, then proofread.
Create

ChatGPT

Drafts, rewrites, summaries, brainstorming and answers to questions. It has a free tier and paid plans for heavier use.

Watch out: it can state wrong facts confidently. Check anything important.

Which should a beginner start with?

If you can only pick one, start with the free version of the one that matches your biggest problem. Blank-page problem: an AI assistant. Mistakes in your writing: Grammarly. Upgrade only when the tool is clearly saving you time on paid work.

A real example: replying to a client

Say a client asks for a revised quote. Here is how the two tools fit together without doing each other's job.

  1. Draft: jot down the price, deadline and one question in rough notes. Ask an AI assistant to turn them into a polite, short email.
  2. Edit: read the draft aloud. Remove anything you would not say, and fix any detail the assistant guessed wrong.
  3. Proofread: let Grammarly catch typos, missing words and tone problems.
  4. Send: you stay responsible for the final message.

When to skip each

  • Skip an AI assistant when the text must be exact and personal, such as a legal notice or a sensitive message, or when you are not allowed to use AI for the work.
  • Skip a proofreader when you rarely write, or when a human editor already checks everything you publish.

What neither tool does

Neither one checks that your facts are right or that your offer makes sense. An assistant can sound confident and still be wrong, and a proofreader can polish a weak argument. The thinking is still yours.

Frequently asked questions

Is Grammarly an AI writing tool?

It uses AI to suggest corrections and rewrites, but its main job is checking text you already wrote. Check its site for current features.

Which one is better for beginners?

Start with the one that matches your biggest problem. Blank page: an assistant. Mistakes in your writing: a proofreader. Both have free options to try.
